# Artwork

[Wide repository banner](banner.png) and [mod-manager cover](thumbnail.png) use the same art direction as Shallow Water Diving: distressed cream/yellow condensed type, charcoal texture, angular yellow framing and cinematic Helldiver artwork.

Both images were generated with the built-in image-generation tool and visually reviewed. The scene shows a helmeted Helldiver mantling a reachable rocky ledge with a usable top. Visible AI-development disclosure is retained. Nonessential PNG metadata is removed without changing the encoded pixels.

## Banner prompt

Use case: ads-marketing. Create an original finished wide 3:1 banner, approximately 2172x724, for a Helldivers 2 mod named "Consistent Vaulting". Art direction: a sibling to the Shallow Water Diving cover: enormous heavy condensed distressed cream and military-yellow block typography on the left, charcoal worn scratched graphic texture, thin yellow angular clipped-corner top and bottom border rules, three yellow hazard slashes at the lower left, cinematic realistic game-art illustration on the right. Text exactly: "Consistent" in cream on the first line, "Vaulting" in yellow beneath, and a small clear footer "AI-assisted development with GPT-6 Astra". No other text, names, usernames or logos. Composition: readable title fills the left 60 percent with generous margins; integrate the scene into the right side of the dark banner. Scene: one fully helmeted Helldiver in black and gray armor with worn yellow accents and a flowing black/yellow cape, actively mantling a clearly reachable waist-to-chest-height broken rocky ledge in a moody ruined alien city. One gloved hand visibly braces on the ledge, one knee rises onto the broad flat walkable top, the other leg trails below in a believable climbing movement. Show both the near ledge face and the usable broad landing surface clearly. This is an ordinary athletic mantle over a low obstacle, not free climbing a vertical cliff, wall running or flying. Correct human anatomy: exactly two arms and two legs. Charcoal rock, weathered concrete, muted storm-gray atmosphere and distant ruined industrial silhouettes, subtle dust and mist, dramatic restrained rim light on armor and edge. No water, swimming, spacecraft, drop pods, weapons firing, other people, arrows, UI, before/after panels, version badges, screenshot identifiers or watermarks. Keep the footer legible and fully within the border. Professional cohesive mod-family cover rather than a collage.
